Lakshman lies gravely wounded. Hanuman must cross the skies to bring back the Sanjeevni Booti from Dunagiri Parvat on Sushen Vaidya's prescription.

Before he sets out, Shri Ram grants him the Ram Kavach to endure the dangers ahead, and Maa Sita blesses him with Seeta Shakti to clear his path.

YOUR MISSION

Fly fast. Gather strength. Bring Sanjeevni home.

FLIGHT GUIDE

  • Fly through gates to restore your Kavach and Shakti.
  • Obstacles drain your shield; use Seeta Shakti to clear them.
